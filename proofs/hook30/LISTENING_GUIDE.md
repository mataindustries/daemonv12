# HOOK//30 listening guide

Two 30-second pieces grown from the B1 hook you picked. Both are D minor, 96 BPM, 12 bars, exactly 30.000 s, at
−16 LUFS (the same level as B1), and both loop without a gap.

**Easiest way to listen on Android:** open the listening page, tap a take, and it loops. Tapping the other take
switches at the same bar, and the page shows which movement, bar and chord you are in. The download buttons save the
MP3 and WAV files to the phone.
<https://claude.ai/artifact/5aa7ca1-placeholder> (private to the owner until shared).

**Files** (in this folder): `HOOK30-<NAME>.wav` is the seamless master. `HOOK30-<NAME>-loop-x3.mp3` plays it three
times so you hear the return. `HOOK30-<NAME>.mp3` is one pass. Stems are in `<NAME>/stems/`.

I composed both and cannot hear them. Every description below is what the score does, not a claim about how it
sounds.

## What was kept from B1

| B1 element | Where it is in HOOK//30 |
|---|---|
| The hook: A4, a cry up to F5, then E5 C5 D5 | Bars 1–4 of both pieces are B1 note for note in voice, bass, mallets and clock. A test compares them |
| The answer a third lower, the climb, the upside-down cadence | Bars 2, 3 and 4, unchanged. The cadence also closes bar 12, so the last note (A4) is the first note of the hook |
| The racing mallets of bar 3 | Heard once in bar 3 exactly as in B1. In bars 11–12 the same eight notes come back split between bars (left) and tines (right), then carry on climbing in triplets, then sixteenths |
| The three-note mallet pickup into bar 1 | Closes bar 12 in both pieces, and announces the return before bar 9 |
| Instruments | The same four (Impossible Voice, Living Bass, mallet bars, clock) plus tines, spring, a second voice, kick and snare from the same rack. ASCENSION adds bowed bars |

## What to listen for

1. **Recognition.** In the first two seconds, is it the B1 hook? Does the new rhythm section help it or crowd it?
2. **The middle.** Does 0:10–0:20 take you somewhere new, or does it feel like a detour? Which event stays with you:
   the bass singing the hook (GROOVE, 0:10), or the two voices in canon over the climbing bass (ASCENSION, 0:10)?
3. **The break.** Is the silence before 0:20 long enough to make you want what comes next? ASCENSION's is longer.
4. **The payoff.** Does 0:20–0:30 feel earned? Does the higher answer at 0:22.5 land?
5. **The mallets.** At 0:25–0:30, can you hear two instruments talking, and do they speed up into the loop?
6. **The return.** Does 0:30 pull you into 0:00? In ASCENSION the piece goes major at 0:20 and is back in minor at
   the loop: does that feel like a fall or like a reset?
7. **Twenty times.** Loop each one for a few minutes. Which one do you stop noticing, and which one annoys you?

Things I would check first, because the numbers cannot tell me:

- The kick sits at about the same level as the bass. If it sounds like a thud under the mix, it is 2–3 dB too quiet;
  if the hook feels pushed aside, it is too loud.
- The lead's highest notes (A5 in GROOVE, B5 in ASCENSION, C6 in both at 0:26.7). They measure no louder than the
  rest of the line, but they may sound thin or sharp.
- The low second voice in GROOVE at 0:12.5 is new. It may sound like a different character or simply too low.
- Both mixes are dark: about 1 % of the energy is above 2 kHz, the same as B1. On a phone speaker the bass
  fundamentals (55–150 Hz) will mostly not be heard; the tines and the bass's upper harmonics carry those lines.

## Rebuild

```bash
node studio-001/scores/hook30/compose.ts
```

```bash
node studio-001/scores/hook30/build.ts NEW_DIR
```

The first writes the two score files from the authoring script. The second renders, loops, masters and verifies
(about 45 s, needs FFmpeg with libmp3lame and flac). Technical details: [README.md](README.md).
