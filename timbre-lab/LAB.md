# Timbre Lab · instrument reference

Generated by `node timbre-lab/docs.ts` (lab 0.1.0). Do not edit by hand.

Two instrument families the Signature Rack could not make, built as new synthesis engines (not presets). They implement the rack's instrument interface and calibration (reference note at macro 0, velocity 0.8 = -20 dBFS RMS) but are **not approved and not frozen**, and they are not in the Composer Kit.

## Using them

The rack's own files are hash-pinned by the v0.8 proof and are not edited. The lab instruments are registered per process, by loading `timbre-lab/register.ts` before the rack:

```bash
node --import ./timbre-lab/register.ts studio-001/rack/cli.ts list
node --import ./timbre-lab/register.ts studio-001/rack/cli.ts render SCORE.json OUT_DIR
node --import ./timbre-lab/register.ts composer-kit/build.ts SPEC.json OUT_DIR
node timbre-lab/audition.ts        # re-render the auditions
```

In a score they are ordinary parts: `{ "id": "drum", "instrument": "organic-percussion/skin", "macro": 0.3, "notes": [[0, 0.5, "D3", 0.9, { "articulation": "bass" }]] }`. From code: `const { rack } = await import("./timbre-lab/index.ts")` before anything else imports the rack. Without the registration nothing changes: the registry, its document and every approved render are byte-identical.

Controls accept a constant, `[[beat, value], ...]` ramps or `{ "steps": [[beat, value], ...], "glide": beats }`, absolute (`controls`) or added to the macro mapping (`offsets`), clamped to the listed range. Velocity is scaled by the part's `expression` as in the rack.

## tight → cavernous · organic-percussion

### `organic-percussion/skin` · Hand skin

A skin over an open shell, played by hands that really collide with it: contact time, brightness and the pitch glide of the stretched skin all follow the hit. A hand that stays damps the note; a wet finger rubbed on the skin moans at the skin's own pitch. One drum per written pitch, all still ringing when the next hit lands.

Version `0.1.0+timbre-lab` · range C2–C5 (MIDI 36–72) · suggested gain -6 dB · macro `tight → cavernous`

| Articulation | Effect |
|---|---|
| `open` | fingers bounce off: the ringing tone |
| `bass` | palm in the centre: low boom, long contact |
| `slap` | fingertips whip the edge and stay a moment: bright crack |
| `mute` | hand stays on the skin for the length of the note: dead, slightly sharp |
| `tap` | one fingertip, soft: ghost note |
| `roll` | finger roll for the length of the note (alternating fingers, swelling) |
| `rub` | wet finger rubbed for the length of the note: a friction moan that follows press/bend |

| Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `press` | -5 … 12 | squeeze on the skin in semitones (talking-drum bend); moves ringing notes too |
| `glide` | 0 … 4 | tension glide: semitones a hard hit starts sharp before the skin settles |
| `damp` | 0.3 … 3 | decay scale (1 = natural; lower rings longer) |
| `shell` | 0 … 0.5 | coupling to the air in the shell: a boom an octave under the tone |
| `buzz` | 0 … 1 | beads resting on the surface: they rattle while it moves enough to throw them |
| `pos` | -0.4 … 0.4 | moves every strike toward the centre/root (-) or the edge/tip (+) |

Setup (per part): `seed` (0 … 4294967295: random seed (hand positions, roll and scrape timing)); `spread` (0 … 1: stereo spread of the bodies, low to high (default 0.5)).

Per-note parameters: `bend` (semitones squeezed over the length of the note (pitch travels while it rings or moans)); `pos` (strike position 0 centre .. 1 edge (replaces the articulation's own)); `slide` (beats: stay on the previous note's body and bend it to this pitch, up to an octave).

### `organic-percussion/log` · Hollow log

A wooden tongue over a hollow box. The air inside is tuned a hair under the tongue, so every note is a slow doublet that wobbles as it dies; the box answers with dry knocks. Mallet mass and hardness decide the contact, not a filter.

Version `0.1.0+timbre-lab` · range C2–G5 (MIDI 36–79) · suggested gain -7 dB · macro `tight → cavernous`

| Articulation | Effect |
|---|---|
| `strike` | soft mallet near the tip of the tongue: round hollow note |
| `knuckle` | hard small knock mid-tongue: bright tok |
| `rim` | on the box edge: dry wooden click, hardly any pitch |
| `mute` | mallet stays on the tongue for the length of the note: choked |
| `scrape` | stick dragged across the ridges for the length of the note, accelerating toward the tip |

| Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `hollow` | 0.002 … 0.12 | how far under the tongue the box air sits: speed of the hollow wobble (fraction of the pitch) |
| `damp` | 0.3 … 3 | decay scale (1 = natural; lower rings longer) |
| `hard` | 0 … 1 | mallet hardness: felt .. bare wood |
| `pos` | -0.4 … 0.4 | moves every strike toward the centre/root (-) or the edge/tip (+) |
| `buzz` | 0 … 1 | beads resting on the surface: they rattle while it moves enough to throw them |

Setup (per part): `seed` (0 … 4294967295: random seed (hand positions, roll and scrape timing)); `spread` (0 … 1: stereo spread of the bodies, low to high (default 0.4)).

Per-note parameters: `pos` (strike position 0 root .. 1 tip (replaces the articulation's own)); `slide` (beats: stay on the previous note's body and bend it to this pitch, up to an octave).

### `organic-percussion/pot` · Clay pot

A clay pot with a side hole. The written pitch is the air inside with the hole open; the playing hand covers and uncovers it, so notes whoop up, sag down or sit dead and low, and fingertips on the wall ring short clay modes over the air.

Version `0.1.0+timbre-lab` · range A1–G4 (MIDI 33–67) · suggested gain -5 dB · macro `tight → cavernous`

| Articulation | Effect |
|---|---|
| `whoop` | palm hits the hole and bounces away: the pitch whoops up to the note |
| `drop` | hit open, then the hand closes the hole over the note: the pitch sags |
| `palm` | palm stays on the hole for the length of the note, then lets go: low dead thud and a small after-whoop |
| `tap` | fingertip on the belly: clay tick with a faint air note |

| Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `wah` | 0 … 1 | hand over the hole (0 open .. 1 covered): bends ringing air |
| `depth` | 0.1 … 0.8 | how far covering the hole lowers the pitch (fraction of the frequency) |
| `damp` | 0.3 … 3 | decay scale (1 = natural; lower rings longer) |
| `air` | 0 … 1 | breath noise of the air rushing through the hole |
| `pos` | -0.4 … 0.4 | moves every strike toward the centre/root (-) or the edge/tip (+) |

Setup (per part): `seed` (0 … 4294967295: random seed (hand positions, roll and scrape timing)); `spread` (0 … 1: stereo spread of the bodies, low to high (default 0.3)).

Per-note parameters: `pos` (tap position 0 neck .. 1 belly); `slide` (beats: stay on the previous note's body and bend it to this pitch, up to an octave).

## whisper → feral · breath-reed

### `breath-reed` · Breath Reed

A reed blown against a bore, simulated: breath pressure, not an envelope, decides whether the note is air, a soft unsteady tone or a hard bark. One player per part (monophonic): legato moves the pitch under continuing breath, the throat growls, the tongue flutters, the pitch scoops, falls, drifts and leans on the breath, and a register vent splits the note into its octave.

Version `0.1.0+timbre-lab` · range A2–C6 (MIDI 45–84) · suggested gain -6 dB · macro `whisper → feral`

| Articulation | Effect |
|---|---|
| `tongued` | the tongue releases the reed: a clear start |
| `legato` | no new attack: the pitch moves under continuing breath (slide in beats, default about 0.04 s) |
| `breathy` | subtone: slow, low pressure, mostly air until the note finds itself |
| `accent` | hard tongue and a pressure spike: the note barks, then settles |
| `growl` | throat growl for this note |
| `flutter` | flutter-tongue for this note |
| `scoop` | starts flat and bends up into the pitch (param bend, semitones, default 2) |
| `fall` | the pitch falls away as the breath lets go (param fall, semitones, default 5) |
| `overblow` | pushed until it breaks into the upper octave over the length of the note |

| Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `breath` | 0 … 1.5 | breath scale (1 = what velocity and expression ask for; under about 0.15 only air; far above 1 the reed chokes); ride it for swells inside a note |
| `bite` | 0 … 1 | embouchure: loose and dark .. hard and bright (reed stiffness and bore brightness) |
| `growl` | 0 … 1 | throat growl sung into the mouthpiece |
| `flutter` | 0 … 1 | flutter-tongue depth |
| `air` | 0 … 1 | breath noise in and around the tone |
| `overblow` | 0 … 1 | register vent: 0 fundamental .. 1 broken into the upper octave, split tones on the way |
| `vib` | 0 … 1.5 | lip vibrato depth (arrives late in a held note) |
| `drift` | 0 … 1 | pitch instability: slow wander and breath-dependent pitch |
| `bend` | -7 … 7 | lip bend in semitones (continuous) |
| `bore` | 0 … 1 | what is heard: the hollow air column (0) .. the buzzing reed itself (1) |

Setup (per part): `seed` (0 … 4294967295: random seed (breath noise, drift, growl)).

Per-note parameters: `bend` (semitones the note starts below its pitch (scoop)); `fall` (semitones the pitch falls over the end of the note); `slide` (beats: legato travel from the previous pitch).

## How they work

- **Organic percussion** (`skin.ts`): a hand or mallet is a mass with soft, stiffening, lossy flesh that collides with a modal body (a circular membrane's Bessel modes, a wooden tongue over box formants, clay wall modes). Contact time and spectrum are consequences of mass, stiffness, position and speed. A hand that stays damps and loads the body; skin tension rises with vibration energy and with a squeeze; an air cavity is a coupled oscillator, solved so the written pitch stays exact; rubbing drives the skin through a stick-slip friction curve; beads resting on the surface leave it and land. One body per written pitch runs for the whole part, so a hit lands on a body that is still moving.
- **Breath reed** (`reed.ts`): breath pressure meets a reed (a pressure-dependent reflection that saturates when the reed beats shut) on a closed-open bore (one delay line with a lossy, inverting open end), at twice the rack rate. Velocity sets how far into the reed's speaking window the player blows; under it there is only air, at its top the reed chokes. One player per part: legato, tongue, throat growl, flutter, lip bends, vibrato and drift all act on the same breath and bore. The `overblow` vent opens a second, shorter path and the note breaks to its twelfth, as on a real cylindrical reed pipe.
- Both are deterministic (seeded noise only) and render faster than real time on one core.
