# CONCRETE GOSPEL V3 · DARK TO DIVINE (16-bar sketch)

16 bars at 144 BPM (half time), F minor. The approved Concrete Gospel V2 drums and 808 are the foundation, unchanged.
The three approved Cathedral Experiment instruments play on top:

- **THRONE//VOLT** on the lead;
- **CATHEDRAL//ENGINE** as the harmony;
- **HEAVEN//MASS** as the choir.

There was one composition pass and one technical QA/fix pass. **Work stops here for the owner's listening review**, before
any full song.

**Nobody has listened yet.** The composer is an AI agent and cannot hear audio. Every musical statement below is design
intent; every number is a signal measurement, not a verdict.

## What changed musically from V2

**Unchanged:**

- the drums and 808 (the same tracks, gains, effects, 808 duck and master chain; the backing is bit-identical to the
  delivered V2);
- the form (16 bars) and where the beat drops, drops out and stops.

**Changed:** everything above the beat.

- **The hook is darker and plain, with no triplet runs.**
  - THRONE//VOLT plays it **an octave lower** than V2's reed (Ab3–F4, peaking once on Ab4).
  - It has **29 notes instead of V2's 44** from the drop on.
  - The call's chromatic triplet run (Ab Bb B → C) becomes **one deliberate bend**, Ab3 gliding up to C4.
  - The answer's triplet run (Eb Db C → Ab) becomes **a leap down a fifth**, Eb4 to Ab3, then the moan home to F3.
  - Accents spike the filter. The sync "scream" is held low, so the lead sounds authoritative rather than squealing.
- **The phrasing runs in four-bar sentences** instead of a repeated two-bar loop:
  - bars 5–6 ask (they stop on an unresolved Db);
  - bars 7–8 answer (the moan home);
  - bars 9–11 climb (C–Eb–F, then **Gb**, the Neapolitan, then the Ab4 peak), and the lead dives with the 808 into the
    hole;
  - bars 13 and 15 are calls only (the choir answers);
  - the very last moan falls **C4 → A3**, onto the major third of the final chord.
- **The harmony is stronger.** V2 is i–iv–V under the hook, with a major answer in bar 12. V3 keeps the 808's roots and
  colours them:
  - Fm9, Db/F, C7♭9 and C7(♭9 ♭13);
  - a **Neapolitan sixth** (Gb/Bb) in bar 10;
  - Bbm9 → C7sus4 → C7♭9 in the hole;
  - a Db lift in the climax;
  - a **minor plagal amen (Bbm) resolving to F major** as the drums stop. The major chord arrives only once, at the end
    ("dark to divine").
- **The organ is a harmonic force, not a lead.**
  - CATHEDRAL//ENGINE answers the lead in its rests: short Swell chords, gospel call and response.
  - It becomes monumental twice: a full-chorus hit when the ceiling lifts in bar 13, and full organ on the last chord.
  - Its Pedal 16'/32' enters only after the 808 has dived away.
- **The choir has an arc.** V2 had three "oo" voices. V3 has a six-line HEAVEN//MASS chorale:
  - one to three voices humming the hook's shape in the dark intro (Fm → Db → C);
  - a few voices joining the organ's answers in bars 9–11;
  - **a cappella** in bar 12, close, on a dominant, then a breath of silence;
  - from the slam, **high and wide** (Fm/Db up to Ab5, a descant on C6–Db6), answering the lead and singing the whole
    answer phrase of bar 14 itself;
  - octavists on F1 only on the last chord.
- **The texture is never all-in until the end.**
  - Each instrument has its own time slot: the lead calls; the organ, then the choir, answers.
  - Each has its own register: the 808 below, the lead in the middle, the choir above.
  - The only moment everything sounds together is the final F major, after the drums stop.
  - There are no risers and no crescendo builds. The tension comes from harmony, silence (bar 12) and register.

## Mix and low end

- **Levels.** One calibration render during composing set each new track's level per section against V2's own stems:
  - the lead at the V2 reed's in-mix loudness (it already was: −17.4 / −16.5 / −16.7 LUFS against the reed's −17.1 /
    −16.9 / −16.4);
  - the organ's answers about 4 LU under the lead;
  - the choir's breath in bar 12 quieter than the groove;
  - the choir in the climax just above the 808.
- **Around the 808.**
  - While the 808 plays, no choir note is below Db3 and the octavists are silent. The low notes (F2, C2, Bb2) are sung
    only where there is no 808: bars 1–4, bar 12 and the last chord.
  - The organ's 16' and 32' stops speak only after the 808 has gone.
  - Basses and organ step aside briefly on every kick and snare (choir basses 4 dB, tenors and altos 2.5, sopranos 1.5,
    organ 2), so V2's punch survives.
  - The Naves have high-passed tails (200–220 Hz).
- **Gain staging.**
  - The final full organ clipped the Great's PCM16 rack stem at −6 dB, so it is rendered 4 dB lower and the engine track
    gets the 4 dB back.
  - The lead is rendered 6 dB hotter at rack stage (fix pass), so its engine track stays within the engine's +12 dB
    range.

### What the pass found and fixed

| # | Found in the candidate | Fix | Before → after |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **The lead was buried in the climax.** During its own notes in bars 13–16 the choir and organ covered it: 0 to −7 dB under the rest of the mix, where V2's reed led by +4 to +6 | The choir steps back further while the lead calls: sopranos 5 dB more in bar 13 and 8 in bar 15; tenors and altos 6 and 9. Under the last answer: sopranos 11, the others and the Swell 9. It opens back up on the F major chord at beat 62. Its own answers and responses are unchanged | Lead over the choir and organ during its calls: +3.4 / +0.8, −0.3 / −1.8, −3.5 / −7.0 → **+11.3 / +8.4, +8.5 / +6.6, +7.7 / +4.1 dB** |
| F2 | The lead's in-mix margin was lower than V2's reed throughout | Lead +1 dB from the drop, +2.5 dB from the slam, +1 dB on the bar-15 call (all measured after its 2:1 compressor) | See the table above |
| F3 | Bar 11: the choir-and-Swell answer covered the lead's falling F4 (−0.3 dB at 300 Hz–1 kHz) | That answer 3 dB down | **+2.4 / +4.4 dB** |

**How the fix pass went:**

- F2 was first written as a post-mix gain (+1 / +2.5 / +4.5 dB). The engine applies track gain *before* the lead's 2:1
  compressor, and the result exceeded the engine's +12 dB track range.
- The second attempt doubled the steps. It drove the bar-15 accent (a resonant filter spike that passes the compressor's
  12 ms attack) to +1.06 dBFS on the track: 2 clipped samples. The build's stem check caught it.
- The final values were then chosen by running the lead's exact engine chain offline, with the same filters and gain
  curve (it reproduced the +1.06 dBFS exactly). The bar-15 call got +1 dB instead of +2.5, and the extra margin there and
  on the last answer comes from the deeper choir dips.

**Calibration before the candidate (part of composing, not the fix pass):**

- The first render had the choir and organ 7–11 dB too hot in the climax: the sopranos' bus −10.0 LUFS, and the Great
  −12.9 (−5.5 on the last chord), against the 808's −16.7.
- The organ's answers were too faint (−27 LUFS in bars 5–8).
- The bar-12 breath was louder than the groove.
- The section levels in [`compose.ts`](compose.ts) were set from that render.

## Decisions and limitations

- **The lead's 300 Hz–1 kHz margin is lower than the reed's (+2.3 to +2.9 dB against +6.2 to +6.6).** This follows from
  the lower register: the fundamentals are now under 300 Hz, where the 808's harmonics live. It leads by +4.2 to +4.9 dB
  at 1–4 kHz, where V2's reed led by +2.5 to +7.5.
- **F1's trade-off.** Under the lead's calls the choir's ceiling sits back 5–11 dB, to about −23 to −25 LUFS. It is
  enormous in its own answers and on the last chord, not under the lead.
- **The two phrase-level climax readings in the final QA (+2.0 / +0.5 and −0.3 / −1.9 dB)** include the choir's answers
  and the final chord, where the lead's last A3 is meant to dissolve into the chord. The row restricted to the lead's own
  notes is the measurement of the fix.
- **The first beat.** The intro's first beat is the bell and a single humming bass (the "swell" onset reaches −50 dBFS
  51 ms after the beat).
- **Reproducibility.**
  - Per the brief, there was no double-build reproducibility run.
  - The pipeline is deterministic (seeded instruments, the same engine).
  - The final build was run twice (the second time only to change the stem writer), and both listening files came out
    byte-identical.
- **Type check.** The sources pass `tsc` with the repository's strict settings, checked with a temporary config that is
  not committed.

## Things only listening can decide

1. **THRONE//VOLT, an octave down.**
   - Is it authoritative, or too dark to cut?
   - Do the bend and the fifth leap command like a preacher, or has the hook lost what made it memorable?
   - Is +2.5 dB in the climax too much?
2. **CATHEDRAL//ENGINE.**
   - Do the short answers in the rests read as call and response, or as interruptions?
   - Is the bar-13 full-chorus hit monumental, or too much?
3. **HEAVEN//MASS.**
   - Is the hummed intro intimate, or too dark and quiet?
   - Does the a-cappella dominant and silence of bar 12 lift the slam more than V2's major answer did?
   - In bars 13–16, does the ceiling still feel enormous with the choir stepping back under each call?
   - Does the hush under the last answer, then the bloom into F major, feel like a cathedral opening, or like ducking?
4. **The ending.** Is a Picardy F major with the lead's moan landing on A divine, or too sweet for a dark trap record?
5. **Next.** What should the full song keep, and what should it change?

## Rebuild

Requirements:

- Node 22.18+ (tested with 22.23.5);
- FFmpeg 6.1.1 with libmp3lame;
- for the QA, Python 3 with numpy and scipy.

```bash
node experiments/concrete-gospel-003/build.ts --candidate
```

```bash
python3 -I experiments/concrete-gospel-003/qa/measure.py experiments/concrete-gospel-003/build --json experiments/concrete-gospel-003/build/qa-candidate.json
```

```bash
node experiments/concrete-gospel-003/build.ts
```

```bash
PYTHON=python3 node experiments/concrete-gospel-003/deliver.ts
```

- `build.ts` rebuilds and verifies the V2 backing the first time (through `../cathedral-experiment-001/backing.ts`).
  It then renders the five rack scores in parallel and the engine project, and writes the listening files and stems.
  It takes about 70 s on 4 cores.
- `deliver.ts` runs the final QA and the freeze check, then fills `delivery/`.
