# SAMPLE LAB 02 · Sources, generations and provenance

Every sound in the three sketches is generated by code in this folder: Node built-ins, fixed xorshift32 seeds and the
DaemonV12 engine. Nothing is downloaded, sampled, borrowed from a pack, or recorded from a third party. The
machine-readable version, with full SHA-256 hashes and every input of every render, is
[`sources.manifest.json`](sources.manifest.json), written by `scripts/build.ts`. This is evidence, not legal advice.

**What "recording" means here.** This lab had no microphone, so the source "recordings" are physical and acoustic
models of real things (a radio receiver, a mug, water, a whistler in a stairwell). Each is built from the physics of its
source rather than from a synthesizer preset, and each take contains several performances with different strength,
position and timing, like a session. They are original, but they are not field recordings. Phone recordings of real
objects could replace any generation-0 file. Only the chop times in the source scripts would need re-annotating.

The kitchen and the stairwell are synthetic rooms from `dsp.ts` (early reflections into an 8-line feedback delay
network). Every build regenerates generation 0 twice and fails unless both runs match the committed bytes.

**The announcer** is a Klatt-style cascade formant synthesizer written for this lab (`voice.ts`): a band-limited
KLGLOTT88 glottal source, five formants, a nasal pole-zero pair and a frication branch. It is not a recording, clone or
imitation of any real person. Its words are radio procedure words. **The tunes** (the interval signal, the whistled
melody, every hook, bassline and drum part) were written for this lab.

| File | Project | What the engine does | SHA-256 (first 16) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mug-quarter.wav` | `projects/mug-quarter.json` | The first tap of `mug.wav` at playback rate 0.25: A5 → A3, 1.8 s → 7.2 s | `5cc7a2f4f483081d` |
| `whistle-quarter.wav` | `projects/whistle-quarter.json` | The steady start of the held B5 in `stairwell.wav` (4.75–5.10 s) at rate 0.25: B5 → B3 | `1a1f607cbd0aa321` |

The sketches then play each print at quarter speed again, so both basses are their source four octaves down and
sixteen times slower.
