lighting plates and camera design were then locked; the master uses the same frame
function at the required resolution and frame rate.

## Review findings

The ceramic substrate, machined copper, plated vias, package leads and silicon tiles
read as one fabricated physical environment. The oblique composition and restrained
dolly preserve this environment. Cyan surge heads visibly change position along
connected traces; separate amber apertures follow the processor's own axes and change
with syllables; fine branch activity becomes denser toward overload. Dark regions and
material reflections remain readable. No waveform, particle field, holographic display
or circular energy core is used. The final loss of every layer reads as a deliberate
punctuation.

The first proof exposed insufficient signal contrast and gray outside-board background.
Compositing exposure, aperture slots, local emission and the board matte were corrected
using the same scene plates. The updated proof passed review before the full master.
No geometry or lighting rebuild occurred after the motion proof.

The full master is subject to independent stream, PCM, event-onset, terminal-pixel and
determinism checks recorded in `products/verification.md`.
