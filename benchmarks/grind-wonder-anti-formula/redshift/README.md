## 1 · What Gate 2 actually is

Read from `../c-impossible-ascent-gate2.json`, its render provenance, the seven processed stems and the master.

**The hook.** Phantom (the seven-voice ghost-vowel lead, root E5) climbs **E5 – F♯5 – A5 – B5** in four even
quarter notes at 144 BPM: steps of +2, +3, +2 semitones, the B cut short to a dotted eighth. Then comes the
**fall-back**: one sixteenth F♯5 on the bar's last 16th, a falling fourth that says *not yet*. The pitch set
{0, 2, 5, 7} has no third. What makes it recognisable is the even quarter-note climb, that open, third-less shape, and
the tiny falling-fourth pickup.

**Its transformations.** The phrase is stated 11 times in 28 s:
- p2 (bar 3): eighths, with the wrong note F♮ (♭2), reaching C♯6.
- p3 (bar 5): sixteenths through D♯ to E6, then dragged back down by a glide.
- bar 7: a two-note fragment.
- bar 8: the tritone rupture B♭–C–D♯–F, gliding down.
- bars 11–12: augmented.
- bar 13: compressed, with octave pops.
- bar 14: E6–C♯–B, left unresolved.
- bar 15: p1 again, over chords.
- bar 16: one unbroken glide E→B→E6.
- bar 17: a last E6.

**Where phrases are interrupted.** In movement 1 every attempt is answered by the wall: the low boom at about E0,
on the downbeats of bars 2, 4 and 6. No phrase reaches the next downbeat. p1 falls back to F♯, p2 falls to A,
p3 is dragged down, bar 7 stops after two notes, bar 8 ruptures, bar 9 is a −45 dB hole, and bar 17 slams and
empties out.

**What makes the breakthrough work.**
1. The bar-9 hole (−45.4 dB RMS) is the springboard for the dry 808 in bar 10 (−13.1 dB), a jump of 32 dB.
2. The low end starts to move: until bar 10 it is a static Abyss drone and the wall; then the CRUSHed
   Abyss/Reactor 808 arrives.
3. The only upward glides in the piece come in bar 16 (one continuous gesture). Every other glide drags something down.
4. The obstacle gives way: in bar 17 the wall glides down an octave.

**Strongest contrast.** Movement 1 is essentially two voices with nothing between them: the phrase high, the wall
and drone in the sub, one kick at a time. Movement 3 is CRUSHed trap. The most extreme single moment is bars 8 → 9 → 10, measuring
−13.9 → −45.4 → −13.1 dB.

**What would turn repetitive if extended.**
1. Another stepwise climb; it already happens 11 times.
2. Downward glides as "gravity": p3, the rupture, the wall in bars 8 and 17, the 808 drop in bar 17.
3. A phrase that stops, then silence and one isolated sound (bars 7, 9 and 17).
4. Movement 3's displaced groove: late or doubled kicks, snares shifted by a 16th, triplet hat stumbles. A seventh
   bar of it would be a loop.
5. The E1 808 pedal, which is about 80 % of the 808 notes.
6. 28 s of open fifths: G and G♯ never sound.
7. The wall on even downbeats.

**Two small facts this section is built on.**
- Gate 2's kick and snare play at rate 1, so the kick is A1 and the snare A3: a fourth above the E world.
- The hats' "stumble" is a 144 BPM triplet eighth, 0.139 s.

## 2 · The one decision: REDSHIFT

*The breakthrough changes gravity.* In strong gravity, clocks run slow and light arrives redder (lower). So past
the wall **the whole world plays at three-quarter time and three-quarter rate: 108 BPM, a fourth lower, every
envelope 4/3 longer. The one exception is the phrase that broke through. It keeps its own proper time: Wonder's
p1, note for note, still 144 BPM, still in E.**

Everything below follows from that one rule.

- **The world is Gate 2's own instruments at ×0.749 (−5 semitones).**
  - kick A1 → E1, snare A3 → E3, wall boom rate 0.5 → 0.375, glass E5 → B4;
  - the 808 vocabulary E1 floor / E2 pops / B1 → B0 / B1 / F♯1;
  - even the 808's sidechain duck is stretched by 4/3.
  
  No new instruments, no new samples.
- **One pulse, two clocks.** A 108 BPM sixteenth is exactly a 144 BPM triplet eighth: Gate 2's hat stumble. The
  stumble becomes the floor. The unchanged phrase (even quarters at 144) therefore lands as dotted eighths against
  the slower world, four notes in three world beats.
- **Same notes, three meanings.**
  - The first statement (28.33 s) starts together with the world.
  - The second (31.67 s) starts on the world's snare.
  - The world's next downbeat (32.78 s) cuts between its A and B.

  The two clocks only agree every 6.67 s (4 Gate 2 bars = 3 world bars): at 28.33, 35.00 and 41.67 s.
- **35.00 s, the lock.** Gravity captures the phrase's *downbeat*: from here each statement starts on a world bar.
  It does not capture its *inner time*: the notes still come every 0.417 s. The 808 and kick hit with every phrase
  note, and the 808 sings the phrase a fourth below. For the first time the floor sings the hook. The phrase's own
  rhythm becomes the groove's syncopation; nothing new was written for it.
- **37.22 s.** The Phantom is silent and the world sings the phrase without its singer: the 808, plus the glass on
  B4–C♯5–E5–F♯5.
- **39.44 s, last statement.** At the summit (40.69 s) the floor drops to B0, and the little fall-back F♯ is held.
  In E it meant *fell back*; over the new floor it is the fifth, so it lands.
- **41.67 s.** Both clocks agree again, and there is nothing there. The expected downbeat never comes.

**Wonder's material vs. mine.**
- *Wonder's*: the phrase (p1, verbatim, four statements), every sample, the roles of the sounds, the 144 BPM
  grid, the CRUSH recipe and the master chain.
- *Mine*: the gravity rule (3:4 in time *and* pitch for everything but the phrase), the two-clock form and its
  lock, the redshifted world groove, the floor singing the phrase, and the held fall-back ending.

**Deliberate things you may notice.**
- The world sounds slower, deeper and darker than Gate 2: every world sound plays at 0.749, and the 808 is
  low-passed at 3 kHz.
- The phrase seems to rush over it in cycle 1.
- Nothing in the extension glides, and nothing new climbs. Every ascent is Wonder's phrase, sung by the Phantom or,
  in the lock, doubled a fourth below by the floor and the glass.
- G/G♯ still never sounds.
- The piece now ends on B, not E: the new gravity's floor.

## 3 · The join

Gate 2's master is cut at exactly 28.0 s while its last glass note is still ringing in the atmosphere room
(about −50 dBFS). Plain concatenation would put a step of −50/−49 dBFS there, a faint click in near-silence.

Instead, `gate2_tail.ts` re-renders **Wonder's unchanged project** with only `render.duration` lengthened, and runs
**Wonder's unchanged `mix.ts`** on it. The temporary copy differs only in five path expressions, plus one appended
line that records three gains. Results:

- All seven processed Gate 2 stems come out **byte-identical** over the first 28.0 s.
- The longer master differs only in its last 60 frames (27.9986–28.0 s), by at most −69 dBFS. That is Gate 2's
  oversampled soft clipper looking ahead into the cut.
- What follows 28.0 s is therefore Gate 2's own glass and room decay, not a reconstruction.
- A 5 ms correction, faded out on the extension side, makes it continuous with the approved last sample.

The combined WAV carries **Gate 2's PCM bytes unchanged** (checked). The step at the join is −71/−61 dBFS (L/R),
smaller than the signal's own largest sample-to-sample step in the preceding 10 ms (−60/−56 dBFS). The first new
event arrives exactly one Gate 2 bar after Gate 2's last downbeat.

No Gate 2 sample was edited, so no separate "edited endpoint" version was needed.

## 5 · Files and reproduction

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `build_redshift.py` | The score: writes `../c-impossible-ascent-redshift.json` and `score/redshift.events.{json,md}` |
| `gate2_tail.ts` | Gate 2's natural tail for the join, plus the three Gate 2 gains (`audio/gate2-reference.json`) |
| `mix_redshift.ts` | Mix using Gate 2's recipe: `audio/` masters, stems, `REPORT.json` |
| `verify_redshift.ts` | Quality gate: `audio/QA.json` |
| `source-render/` | Render provenance (JSON/MIDI). The WAVs there are reproducible intermediates and not committed (same convention as Gate 2) |

```sh
python3 benchmarks/grind-wonder-anti-formula/redshift/build_redshift.py
node bin/daemonv12.js render benchmarks/grind-wonder-anti-formula/c-impossible-ascent-redshift.json \
  --out-dir benchmarks/grind-wonder-anti-formula/redshift/source-render --stems --format wav
node benchmarks/grind-wonder-anti-formula/redshift/gate2_tail.ts      # needs FFmpeg, ~3.5 min
node benchmarks/grind-wonder-anti-formula/redshift/mix_redshift.ts    # needs FFmpeg
node benchmarks/grind-wonder-anti-formula/redshift/verify_redshift.ts --full
```

The project spans bars 1–25 of Gate 2's own 144 BPM grid, with bars 1–17 deliberately empty. That way every event
lands on the frame Gate 2's renderer would give it, and the mix keeps frames from 28.0 s.

The engine, instruments, samples, motif layer, DAEMON//CRUSH and all approved masters are unchanged. No visuals,
no alternatives, no further sections.
