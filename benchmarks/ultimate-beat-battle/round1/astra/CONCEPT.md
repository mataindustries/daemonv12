# Pass 1 — concept

Written before arranging.

- Hook: a compact D–F–E-to-D rubber-reed call. A lower, dry answer makes the lead feel like a conversation. The opening exposes its consonants immediately.
- Rhythmic identity: 138 BPM broken garage, delayed off-sixteenths and a 3+3+2 cell, with snares that occasionally refuse the expected backbeat.
- Low end: a short, centered pitched body; bass answers the reed and makes room for a separate short kick. No sustained low-frequency bed.
- Structural surprise: the rhythm opens into suspended electric-piano harmony, then a brief vacuum changes the meaning of the returning hook.
- Payoff: the same recognisable phrase comes back brighter and more conversational, followed by a composed final punctuation and a small echo.

The intended form is sixteen 4/4 bars, 27.826 seconds. Sound and arrangement decisions will be revised against the actual DaemonV12 renders. No human listening is claimed.
