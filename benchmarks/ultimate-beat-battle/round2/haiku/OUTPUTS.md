## Reproduce the final render

```sh
cd /home/user/daemonv12
source .agents/env.sh        # DAEMONV12_* paths and the headless FluidSynth wrapper
node benchmarks/ultimate-beat-battle/round2/haiku/build-project.mjs
node bin/daemonv12.js validate benchmarks/ultimate-beat-battle/round2/haiku/project.json --json
node bin/daemonv12.js render benchmarks/ultimate-beat-battle/round2/haiku/project.json \
  --out-dir benchmarks/ultimate-beat-battle/round2/haiku/renders/final-a --stems --format wav,mp3 --json
```

`.agents/env.sh` sets `SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy` and points `DAEMONV12_FLUIDSYNTH` at `/tmp/daemonv12-fluidsynth-headless` (created from the brief's wrapper), `DAEMONV12_SOUNDFONT` and `DAEMONV12_FFMPEG` at the pinned audio tools. The wrapper is not needed for this sample-only project, but doctor expects it.
