# Musical thesis (written before any project JSON)

- **HOOK:** a "talking steel" voice — struck metal that sings a three-note call
  (G–B♭–A♭↘G, grouped 3+3+2 sixteenths) at 0.00 s, answered by a sub that dives an
  octave like an electro zap.
- **RHYTHMIC IDENTITY:** 134 BPM UK-garage 2-step skip (kick 1 and 3+, snare 2 and 4,
  swung sixteenths on machine ticks) under an electro 3+3+2 call; in the build the
  call becomes a 3-against-4 gear cycle.
- **LOW-END ROLE:** the sub is the *answer*, not the downbeat — it speaks only in the
  gaps the call leaves, until the payoff where the bass takes the call itself.
- **CONTRAST IDEA:** bar 9 "stall": the gear cycle seizes, the voice tape-stops two
  octaves into a steel groan, then true silence before the downbeat.
- **PAYOFF:** role swap — vowel bass + sub sing the hook while the steel voice answers
  with the dive the sub used to own; the answer chord moves from ♭VI to ♭II.
- **ENDING:** call and answer finally sound together in octaves, cadence D→G on one
  unison steel hit, short ring, restart on the opening call.
