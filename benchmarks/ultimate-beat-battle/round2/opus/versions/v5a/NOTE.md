# V5a — rejected experiment

Manual sidechain: gain automation ducking **sub and vowel bass** −9 dB at every kick that
lands on a sounding bass note (bar 9 onward), linear recovery over a thirty-second note,
plus a −1 dB master trim. Measured (`analysis/v5a-report.json`): kick led the first 30 ms
of each payoff downbeat (35–130 Hz kick −18.6 vs sub −21.3 / bass −26.4 dB), but the
trim-compensated composite downbeat peak fell from −12.2 (V4) to −13.4 dBFS and the duck
softened the onset of the bass call, which carries the hook in the payoff. Reverted.
